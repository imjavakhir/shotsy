Shotsy — Photo Organizer

Shotsy ("the App") helps you sort, clean up and organize the photos on your iPhone. It is built so that your photos and everything learned from them stay on your device. This policy explains how information is handled.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Information Collection and Use

Shotsy has no account and no sign-in. You are never asked for a name, email address or phone number in order to use it.

Your photos are analyzed on your device. Shotsy never uploads your photos or videos, the text found in your screenshots, faces, names you type, or anything else it learns from your library.

We do not:

  • Require user accounts
  • Upload your photos, videos or screenshots to any server
  • Use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • Collect your name, email address, phone number or location
  • Show advertising, use advertising identifiers, or track you across apps and websites
  • Sell or share data with data brokers

Photo Library Access

Shotsy asks for access to your photo library so it can show, sort, group and search your photos. You can give full or limited access, and change it at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Shotsy changes your library only when you choose an action, such as adding photos to an album, marking a favorite, or saving a compressed copy of a video
  • Photos are deleted only after you review them and confirm in Apple's own system prompt. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where they can be recovered for about 30 days
  • If you use iCloud Photos, Apple may download an original from iCloud when you open, share or compress it. That download is handled by Apple, not by Shotsy

On-Device Analysis

To find similar photos, possible duplicates, blurry shots, large videos and screenshot text, Shotsy uses Apple's on-device frameworks (Vision). The following are stored only on your device:

  • Photo identifiers (references to items in your library, not copies of the photos)
  • Your review decisions (keep, delete, skipped) and sorting progress
  • Image similarity data and sharpness scores used for grouping
  • Text recognized in screenshots, used only for search in the App
  • Smart Collections and settings you create

The analysis cache is excluded from device backups and can be cleared at any time in Settings → Clear analysis cache. Deleting the App permanently removes all of it.

People

The optional People feature detects faces on your device so you can tag people yourself. Face locations and any names you type stay on your device. Shotsy does not identify anyone, does not use face recognition services, and never sends face data anywhere. You can delete all People data in the App at any time.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the App prepares the selected photos and hands them to the iOS share sheet. Where they go next is your choice, and is covered by the app or service you share to, not by this policy.

Notifications and Reminders

  • Reminders are scheduled on your device only
  • No reminder content is sent to external servers
  • Notification permission is used only for the reminders you turn on

In-App Purchases

Shotsy offers optional In-App Purchases to unlock Shotsy Pro (a weekly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase).

  • Payments are processed securely by the Apple App Store
  • Shotsy does not collect or store payment or billing information
  • We do not have access to your financial details
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings

Third-Party Services

Shotsy uses one third-party service:

  • RevenueCat — manages purchases. It receives the purchase receipt and basic device information in order to tell the App whether Shotsy Pro is active, and identifies your installation with a randomly generated anonymous ID. You are not asked to sign in, and no name, email address or photo data is attached to it

RevenueCat is operated by a company based in the United States, so the purchase data described above may be processed there.

Data Security

Your photos and everything derived from them remain under your control on your device. Uninstalling the App permanently removes all data it has stored. Your photos themselves stay in the Photos app.

Children's Privacy

Shotsy does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and is suitable for users of all ages.
